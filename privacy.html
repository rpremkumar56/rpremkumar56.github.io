Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how DS Softwares ("we") handles personal data in BookAssist, our clinic booking software, and in its WhatsApp messages.

Who controls the data

Each clinic that uses BookAssist controls its own patient and staff data. The clinic decides what to record and who may see it. We provide and maintain the software on the clinic's behalf.

What BookAssist stores

  • Patient name and phone number.
  • Appointments: date, time, chair, doctor and attendance.
  • Short treatment notes and the next follow-up date.
  • Staff names, email addresses and roles.
  • Doctors' WhatsApp numbers, when a clinic turns on WhatsApp notices.

BookAssist does not store payment details.

WhatsApp messages

BookAssist sends messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform by Meta. A doctor receives a daily summary of their appointments only after the clinic adds the doctor's number and turns the summary on. When a doctor asks for the full list, the reply contains the appointment times, chairs, patient names and visit reasons for that day.

We keep a log of each message's delivery status. The log holds no patient names. We delete it after 90 days. Meta processes messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India. Connections are encrypted. Encrypted backups are kept for 30 days.

Who can see the data

Only the clinic's signed-in staff. We access a clinic's data only to fix, maintain or back up the system. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

Patients can ask their clinic for a copy, correction or deletion of their record. A doctor can ask the clinic to stop WhatsApp summaries at any time. You can also contact us, and we will pass the request to the clinic.
