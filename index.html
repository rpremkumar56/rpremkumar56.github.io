Software for small clinics

Simple booking and patient reminders, made for the front desk.

DS Softwares builds phone-first tools that replace the paper register at small clinics. Staff book visits, run the day's queue and keep follow-ups on track, without training or new hardware.

BookAssist

BookAssist is our clinic booking app. It runs in the browser and installs on a phone's home screen.

  • Chair calendar. See booked and free slots for each chair and each doctor.
  • Live queue. Mark arrivals and no-shows. When a treatment runs long, later visits move and staff see who to inform.
  • Visit notes and follow-ups. Record the plan and the next visit date, so no follow-up is forgotten.
  • WhatsApp notices (coming soon). A morning summary of the day for each doctor, then patient reminders.
  • Insights. Visits, waiting times and chair use, with filters.
